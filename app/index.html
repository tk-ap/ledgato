ONE-MINUTE GUIDE
Ledgato is a safety check for AI agents.
  1. Choose an attempted action.Tell Ledgato who is acting, what they want to use, and what they want to reach.
  2. Ledgato checks the rule.The policy says what the agent may and may not do.
  3. Ledgato tries to break the boundary.It runs several tests to make sure the forbidden action cannot sneak through another way.
  4. You get a plain answer and proof.The action is allowed or stopped, and Ledgato records evidence showing why.
This preview makes real policy decisions, but it does not connect to or change an outside system.
